## Platform

## Stack

delegated: Vite + React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + GSAP (Vercel-ready)

## Users

Three balanced audiences:

- **Trace-supply partners:** Companies running AI agents in production (starting with coding agents) that want to safely filter, license, and share in the upside of their production traces without giving up ownership of their underlying data or core business.
- **Frontier AI labs:** Model developers and research teams seeking high-signal reinforcement learning environments and ungameable verifiers derived from real-world agent failures rather than synthetic tasks.
- **Research engineering candidates:** Exceptional engineers and researchers evaluating San Francisco Data for in-person roles in San Francisco's Financial District.

## Product Purpose

Present San Francisco Data's thesis, technical pipeline, commercial model, team pedigree, and open roles so trace-supply partners, frontier labs, and research engineers quickly grasp what the company does and take their next step (reading the thesis essay, exploring open roles on Ashby, or contacting the founders).

## Positioning

San Francisco Data turns real production traces from deployed AI agents—where models fail on real economic work—into reproducible reinforcement learning environments and verifiers that grade real user intent rather than test loopholes. As models surpass human experts and contractor-generated data becomes a race to the bottom, SF Data connects AI deployed across the economy directly to the training processes that push the frontier ("a Glencore for production traces": raw traces are to training assets what crude oil is to jet fuel).

## Operating Context

- A production trace is the chronology of AI working on a real task: user request, available context, tool calls, and subsequent feedback.
- Production sessions often contain customer information and proprietary material. SF Data does not claim ownership of underlying data; it partners with source companies, handles permissions and filtering, isolates traces containing genuine model limitations (not broken environments or repetitive work), reconstructs reproducible tasks, writes verifiers, and shares proceeds with source companies.
- Today the majority of economically valuable traces come from coding agents, which is SF Data's starting wedge.
- The team operates in person five days a week on Kearny Street in San Francisco's Financial District.

## Product Principles

1. **Lead with technical substance:** Make the production-trace-to-RL-environment pipeline concrete rather than hiding behind vague AI slogans.
2. **Serve all three audiences cleanly:** Give trace-supply partners, frontier labs, and engineering candidates unmistakable paths without cluttering the narrative.
3. **Preserve strict factual integrity:** Every metric, affiliation, and claim traces to verified company sources; never fabricate logos, quotes, or customer names.
4. **Make partner trust explicit:** Clearly communicate data ownership, permissions, filtering, and shared upside for companies licensing traces.
